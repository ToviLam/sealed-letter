A public sketch

A letter that stays closed.

Someone writes a sentence they are not ready to have read. Two people they trust have to agree before it opens. Those two people still never read it.

01

Write it now

The words belong to the writer, and to the person they are for. Not to the people who will later decide the moment.

02

Two people agree

One agreement is not enough. The letter stays shut until a second person stands with the first.

03

Someone else reads

When the time has come, the letter opens for the person it was meant for. The carriers of the decision are not the audience.

Try the seal

This stays in your browser. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent.

  2. Name two people.
  3. Let both of them agree.
